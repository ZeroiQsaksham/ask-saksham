# Frequently asked questions

## What kind of work is Saksham interested in?
Data science, data analytics, machine learning and AI engineering, especially building complete systems end to end: from SQL analysis and model training to deploying an AI assistant on a website.

## Saksham studied Mining Engineering. Why data science and AI?
Alongside his Mining Engineering degree at IIT (ISM) Dhanbad, Saksham built a strong data and programming foundation on his own: a market analyst internship using SQL and BigQuery, 1000+ competitive programming problems solved, a silver medal in the National Mathematics Olympiad, and hands-on projects in machine learning, deep learning and generative AI.

## What makes Saksham's ML work stand out?
He pays attention to the details that make results trustworthy: in his chest X-ray project he split data by patient to prevent data leakage, handled extreme class imbalance with weighted loss, and used Grad-CAM to check that the model looks at the right anatomy. In this AI agent he added guardrails so it only answers from verified information.

## What is Saksham currently working on?
Finishing the full training run of his chest X-ray diagnosis model and improving this AI agent.

## How was this assistant built?
It is a retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) agent built with LangChain, ChromaDB, FastEmbed and the open-weight GPT-OSS 120B model hosted on Groq, served by FastAPI. It searches Markdown files written by Saksham and answers only from them.
